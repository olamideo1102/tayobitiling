Serving Christchurch & Canterbury

Professional tiling, waterproofing and masonry with NZ compliance

Christchurch Tiler delivers licensed, insured tiling services across Christchurch and greater Canterbury. We specialise in bathroom and shower tiling with certified waterproofing membranes, kitchen splashbacks, floor tiling (porcelain, ceramic and natural stone), heated floors and exterior paving designed for local climate and drainage.

  • Bathroom & shower tiling — NZ approved waterproof membranes, certified wet-area installations.
  • Kitchen splashbacks & feature walls — precision cuts and grout matching for a clean finish.
  • Floor tiling & heated floors — porcelain, large-format and natural stone with underfloor systems.
  • Outdoor paving & masonry — patios, pathways and retaining walls built for Christchurch conditions.
  • Repairs & maintenance — regrouting, tile replacement, sealing and anti-slip treatments.
